# Record a Payment on an Account

Money an account paid: cash, a check or a bank transfer. It is recorded as a receipt, and
settles the unit's invoices, oldest first, or the invoice you name first. Money paid ahead is kept
as the unit's credit for later months.

See [Account Balances] and [Invoices].
