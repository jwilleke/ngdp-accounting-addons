# Prepare This Month's Invoices

What each unit owes this month, one row per billing address: its share of the unit's charge and of
the unit's balance brought forward (a credit when paid ahead), and any late charges on the unit's
earlier invoices that are still unpaid. A unit with no billing address shows why, and is not billed.
Nothing is charged until you issue it.

added. An invoice with nothing owed (paid ahead) is kept, and not emailed.
