# Folios

A folio is one stretch of the same owners, with the same shares, on a unit. A unit always has one current folio, and new invoices go on it. When the owners or their shares change, the unit gets a new folio, and the earlier owners' folio keeps its own balance: it stays open while money is owed, and the new owners never carry that debt.

[{Receivables view='folios'}]

## Record on a folio

A payment on an earlier owners' folio is recorded on [Record a Payment on an Account], choosing that folio. These are not money that moved, so they stay outside the ledger:

### Enter a balance brought forward

What a folio owed, or had as credit, before these books. Once per folio.

[{Form id='folio-opening'}]

### Write off a folio's debt

An amount the association gives up. The folio's balance drops by it.

[{Form id='folio-write-off'}]

### Record a lien

A lien filed with the county on a folio whose debt is judged uncollectable. It does not change the balance; money recovered later is an ordinary payment on the folio.

[{Form id='folio-lien'}]

### Void a folio entry

A balance brought forward, write-off or lien entered by mistake.

[{Form id='folio-item-void'}]

See [Account Balances] and [Invoices].
