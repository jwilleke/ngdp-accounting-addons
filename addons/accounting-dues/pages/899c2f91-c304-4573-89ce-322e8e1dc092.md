# Prepare This Month's Invoices

What each billing party owes this month: its share of the account's charge, any late charges on its
earlier invoices that are still unpaid, and its balance brought forward (a credit when paid ahead).
Joint owners at one address are one party. Nothing is charged until you issue it.
