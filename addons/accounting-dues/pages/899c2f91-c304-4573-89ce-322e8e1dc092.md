# Prepare This Month's Invoices

What each account owes this month: its charge, any late charges on earlier invoices that are
still unpaid, and the balance brought forward (a credit when paid ahead). Nothing is charged until
you issue it.

__This month__

[{Dues view='prepare'}]

__Next month__

[{Dues view='prepare' month='next'}]

Check the table for the month you issue, then issue. Tick the late charges box only if the late charges shown should be
added. An invoice with nothing owed (paid ahead) is kept, and not emailed.
