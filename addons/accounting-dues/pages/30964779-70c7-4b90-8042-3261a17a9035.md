# Record a Payment on an Account

Money an account paid: cash, a check or a bank transfer. It is recorded as a receipt, and
settles the invoices of the party billed, oldest first, or the invoice you name first. Money paid
ahead is kept as that party's credit for later months. Name the party when the account bills more
than one.
