# Refund a Receipt

Return money to the person who paid it: all of a receipt, or part. The receipt stays in the ledger;
the refund is recorded as money out of the same fund and category, linked to it.

[{Form id='receipt-refund'}]

Each refund gets its own number, RF-0001 onwards; give it to the payer with the money. Leave the
amount blank to refund everything that is left. A receipt entered by mistake is not refunded: void it on
[Void an Entry] and record it again.

See [Using Accounting].
