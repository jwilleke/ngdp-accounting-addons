# Record an Entry

Record money paid out of a fund: a check for supplies, a bill paid by card. Every entry says why.

Money that came in is a receipt: use [Record a Receipt].

[{Form id='ledger-entry'}]

A posted entry is never edited. To correct one, void it with a reason and post the replacement on
[Void an Entry].
See [Using Accounting].
