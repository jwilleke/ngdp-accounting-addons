# Using Accounting

This page ships with the __accounting__ add-on and is seeded into [{$applicationname}]
when the add-on is enabled.

Accounting keeps the organization's funds and a ledger of money in and out. Money that comes in is
a __receipt__, numbered R-0001 onwards; money paid out is an __entry__; every one says why. A posted
receipt or entry is never edited or deleted: a mistake is voided, with a reason, and recorded again,
so the record of what happened stays.

Every receipt and entry has a __fund__ (what the money is for) and an __account__ (where it sits).
One bank account holds money for several funds. Cash, checks and card-reader receipts land in
__Cash on Hand__ until they go to the bank; ACH, Zelle and bank transfers land in the default bank
account. The forms pick the account by method; choose another when that is wrong.

## Turn it on

Accounting needs ngdpbase 5.18.0 or later, and its forms add-on for the treasurer's screens.

The add-on keeps its data in the site's database, so the database must be on:

```json
{
  "ngdpbase.database.provider": "sqlite",
  "ngdpbase.addons.accounting.enabled": true
}
```

Set `NGDPBASE_DATABASE_KEY` in the site's environment so the database is encrypted.

## Who can do what

The add-on ships a __treasurer__ role. It needs a strong sign-in (a passkey). Grant the role to the
people who keep the books.

## The treasurer's pages

With the __forms__ add-on enabled, the add-on ships these pages. Only the treasurer can see them and
use their forms, and only an administrator can change them.

| Page | For |
| --- | --- |
| [Accounting] | The treasurer's home: balances and a link to every task |
| [Ledger] | Fund balances and the latest entries |
| [Record a Receipt] | Money received: cash, a check, a bank transfer; numbered R-0001 onwards |
| [Refund a Receipt] | Money returned to the payer, all or part; linked to the receipt |
| [Record an Entry] | Money paid out of a fund |
| [Move Money Between Funds] | A transfer; the total stays the same |
| [Void an Entry] | Void a posted receipt or entry with a reason (money out can be replaced in the same step) |
| [Create a Fund], [Rename a Fund], [Close a Fund] | One task each; a fund is closed, never deleted |
| [Set an Account's Opening Balance] | What each account held when the books started; set once |
| [Monthly Summary] | Last month and this month, with CSV downloads |

For now, moving money, voiding, refunding and downloading do not ask you to sign in again: that
step lost what you had typed into the form. It may come back once the form keeps your entries.

The receipt and entry forms offer the categories the site sets in `ngdpbase.addons.accounting.categories`
(a UCOA account code and its label for each). The bank accounts are the ones the site sets in
`ngdpbase.addons.accounting.accounts`; Cash on Hand is always there.

## Showing the ledger on a page

The `Ledger` plugin draws the ledger live for the person viewing the page. Nothing is copied
into the page, and each reader sees only what their permissions allow.

| Markup | Shows | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[{Ledger view='balances'}]` | Each fund's opening balance, money in, money out and balance, with a total | `ledger-summary-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='accounts'}]` | The same money by where it sits: each account's opening balance, in, out and balance, with a total | `ledger-summary-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='entries' max='20'}]` | The latest entries, newest first (`max='0'` for all) | `ledger-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='entries' fund='<fund id>' voided='true'}]` | One fund's entries, including voided ones | `ledger-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='summary' month='previous'}]` | A period: per fund and per account start, in, out and end; per category income and expense | `ledger-summary-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='summary' from='2026-01-01' to='2026-06-30' export='true'}]` | Any period, with CSV download links | the downloads need `ledger-export` |

`month` is `current`, `previous` or `YYYY-MM`. To show the board the summary without the download
links, put it on a page whose `audience` lists the board, without `export='true'`, and grant the
board `ledger-summary-read`: the summary shows no names.

The figures are always current: after every change to the ledger, pages that show it, and the
fund choices in the forms, are drawn again on their next view.

Click a column heading to sort a table; amounts sort by value, and the Total row stays at the bottom.

## Backups

The ledger lives in its own database file, `accounting.db`, in the site's data folder. The site
backup does not include it yet, so ask the site administrator how it is backed up.
