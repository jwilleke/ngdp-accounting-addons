# Using Accounting

How the organization's money is kept on [{$applicationname}], and how the treasurer, the board, owners and donors each use it. This page ships with the __accounting__ add-on.

## What it does

Three add-ons work together. Each site turns on the ones it needs.

- __Accounting__ (the base): the organization's __funds__ and one __ledger__ of money in and out. Money that comes in is a __receipt__, numbered R-0001 onwards; money paid out is an __entry__; every one says why. Cash and checks go to the bank in __deposits__; a check the bank returns is a __bounced check__. A month already reported to the board can be __locked__ so its figures stay true.
- __Invoices__ (`accounting-receivables`): each month's charge for every unit, which the site calls assessments, rent or dues. A unit is billed through __folios__: one per stretch of the same owners, so a sale or any change of owners starts a new one, and the earlier owners' debt stays theirs. One invoice goes to each billing address, payments settle the oldest invoice first, money paid ahead is kept as credit, and late charges are added when the treasurer says so. The units, their owners and their billing addresses come from the accommodation add-on.
- __Donations__ (`accounting-donations`): donations recorded on receipts, with the IRS wording on the receipt slip when the donor got something back; in-kind donations, described but not valued; pledges; donors; and each donor's year-end __donation statement__.

Two things never change:

- __Nothing is edited or deleted.__ A mistake is voided, with a reason, and recorded again, so the record of what happened stays. An issued invoice or statement is kept exactly as issued.
- __Every amount has a fund__ (what the money is for) and an __account__ (where it sits). One bank account holds money for several funds. Cash, checks and card-reader receipts land in __Cash on Hand__ until they are deposited; ACH, Zelle and bank transfers land in the default bank account.

## Who can do what

| Who | Sees | Does | Permission |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| __Treasurer__ | Everything: every receipt, entry, invoice and donor | Records money in and out, deposits, issues invoices and donation statements, locks periods | The __treasurer__ role. It needs a strong sign-in (a passkey). |
| __Board__ | Fund balances and the monthly summary, without names; every unit's account if the site allows it | Reads only | `ledger-summary-read`, and `account-read` for units' accounts. The site grants these to its board. |
| __Owner__ of a unit | Their unit's current folio on their profile: every invoice, what is paid and left, and the balance | Pays as they always have; the treasurer records it | Every signed-in person (`ledger-self`). A past owner keeps seeing their own folio, with what it still owes. |
| __Donor__ | Their own donations by year, and their statements, on their profile | Gives as they always have | Every signed-in person (`ledger-self`) |
| __Administrator__ | The setup | Turns the add-ons on, sets the chart, funds, accounts and wording; grants roles | Admin |

Owners and donors see only their own records, and someone with none sees nothing. The board sees no names unless the site grants it more.

## How to use it

### Once, when the books start

1. The administrator turns the add-ons on and grants the __treasurer__ role.
2. The treasurer creates the funds on [Create a Fund] (General, Building, …), each with its opening balance.
3. The treasurer sets each account's opening balance on [Set an Account's Opening Balance], from the bank statement and the cash count. Added up, the accounts equal the funds.
4. For invoices: every unit needs a billing address (set by an owner or the treasurer in the accommodation add-on). A unit with none is never invoiced to a guessed address.

### When money comes in

- __A dues or assessment payment:__ [Record a Payment on an Account]. It becomes a receipt and settles the unit's oldest invoice, or the one you name.
- __A donation:__ [Record a Donation]. Pick the donor, or add them first on [Add a Donor]. If they got something back (a dinner, a ticket), enter its value: the receipt slip then shows what is deductible. A donation toward a pledge names the pledge.
- __Anything else__ (a sale, a grant, a refund from a vendor): [Record a Receipt].
- __One check for several things__, such as an assessment and a donation: one receipt with two lines.

Print a receipt's slip from its link when the payer wants one.

### When money goes out

- [Record an Entry]: money paid out of a fund, and why.
- [Move Money Between Funds]: a transfer; the total stays the same.
- [Refund a Receipt]: money returned to a payer, all or part.

### Each month

1. __Invoices:__ on [Prepare This Month's Invoices], check each unit's charge, any late charges, and the balance brought forward. Then issue them; tick email to send each to the unit's owners, and late charges only if the ones shown should be added. A site can instead turn on the scheduled run (Admin → Scheduled Jobs → Resume), which issues and emails them on the 1st by itself.
2. __Deposits:__ when you take cash and checks to the bank, record it on [Make a Deposit]: tick the receipts and enter the total from the slip. When the bank statement shows it, mark it cleared on [Mark a Deposit Cleared].
3. __The board:__ the [Monthly Summary] shows last month and this month by fund, account and category, with CSV downloads for the accountant.
4. __Lock the month__ on [Lock a Period] once it is reported. Nothing dated in it can then change; a mistake found later is fixed with an entry dated today.

### At year end

1. [Donation Statements] shows each donor's year. Issue the statements on [Issue Statements] in January; tick email to send each to the donor's address. A statement is the donor's written acknowledgment for the IRS.
2. A donation voided or refunded after its statement was issued flags the statement; reissue it on [Reissue a Statement], with the reason.

### When something is wrong

| What happened | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| A receipt or entry was recorded wrongly | [Void an Entry], with the reason, then record it again |
| An invoice was issued wrongly | [Void an Invoice], with the reason; the month can then be issued again |
| A deposit was entered wrongly | [Undo a Deposit]; its receipts go back to Cash on Hand |
| A deposit was marked cleared by mistake | [Take Off a Cleared Mark] |
| The bank returned a check | [Record a Bounced Check]; money toward it later goes on [Repay a Bounced Check], and [Bounced Checks] shows what is owed |
| An earlier owners' folio will never be paid | Write it off, or record the lien filed with the county, on [Folios] |
| A folio needs what it owed before these books | Enter its balance brought forward on [Folios] |
| A fund is no longer used | [Close a Fund]; its history stays |
| A locked month needs a change | Rarely: [Unlock a Period], with the reason |

## The treasurer's pages

Only the treasurer can see these and use their forms; only an administrator can change them. The invoice and donation pages are there when those add-ons are on.

| Page | For |
| --- | --- |
| [Accounting] | The treasurer's home: balances and a link to every task |
| [Ledger] | Fund balances and the latest entries |
| [Deposits] | What went to the bank, each with its receipts, to match the bank statement |
| [Monthly Summary] | Last month and this month, with CSV downloads |
| [Invoices] | Every invoice, what is paid and left |
| [Account Balances] | Each unit's balance, or the credit it has paid ahead |
| [Folios] | Each folio of each unit, current or earlier owners', with its balance; a balance brought forward, write-off or lien is recorded here |
| [Donations] | The donations home: pledges, in-kind donations, donors and statements |
| [Pledges], [In-kind Donations] | Promises to give, and donations of goods or services |

The forms offer the categories in `ngdpbase.addons.accounting.categories`: a starter chart that follows QuickBooks (4000s income, 5000s cost of goods, 6000s–7000s expenses, 8000s other income), which the site can change. Receipts offer the income categories; money paid out offers the rest. The bank accounts are the ones the site sets in `ngdpbase.addons.accounting.accounts`; Cash on Hand is always there.

For now, moving money, voiding, refunding and downloading do not ask you to sign in again: that step lost what you had typed into the form. It may come back once the form keeps your entries.

## Showing it on a page

The plugins draw live for the person viewing the page. Nothing is copied into the page, and each reader sees only what their permissions allow.

| Markup | Shows | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[{Ledger view='balances'}]` | Each fund's opening balance, money in, money out and balance, with a total | `ledger-summary-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='accounts'}]` | The same money by where it sits: each account's balance, with a total | `ledger-summary-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='entries' max='20'}]` | The latest entries, newest first (`max='0'` for all), with receipt numbers and deposits | `ledger-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='entries' fund='<fund id>' voided='true'}]` | One fund's entries, including voided ones | `ledger-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='deposits'}]` | Deposits, newest first, with their receipts (`voided='true'` adds undone ones) | `ledger-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='summary' month='previous'}]` | A period: per fund and per account start, in, out and end; per category income and expense | `ledger-summary-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='summary' from='2026-01-01' to='2026-06-30' export='true'}]` | Any period, with CSV download links | the downloads need `ledger-export` |
| `[{Receivables view='prepare'}]` | This month's charges by unit and billing address (`month='next'`, `'previous'` or `YYYY-MM`) | `ledger-read` |
| `[{Receivables view='invoices' month='2026-10'}]` | Invoices, newest first (blank month: all) | `account-read` or `ledger-read` |
| `[{Receivables view='balances'}]` | Each unit: invoiced, paid, balance or credit | `account-read` or `ledger-read` |
| `[{Receivables view='folios'}]` | Each folio: its owners' stretch, current or earlier, balance, and what is recorded on it | `account-read` or `ledger-read` |
| `[{Donations view='pledges'}]` | Open pledges: pledged, given, left | `ledger-read` |
| `[{Donations view='inkind'}]` | In-kind donations: donor, date, description | `ledger-read` |
| `[{Donations view='statements' year='2026'}]` | Each donor's year: totals and statement | `ledger-read` |

`month` is `current`, `previous` or `YYYY-MM`. To show the board the summary without the download links, put it on a page whose `audience` lists the board, without `export='true'`, and grant the board `ledger-summary-read`: the summary shows no names. To show the board every unit's account, grant it `account-read` and use the Receivables `invoices` or `balances` view.

The figures are always current: after every change, the pages that show it are drawn again on their next view. Click a column heading to sort a table; amounts sort by value, and the Total row stays at the bottom.

## Turning it on

Accounting needs ngdpbase 5.31.0 or later, the site's database on, and the forms add-on for the treasurer's screens:

```json
{
  "ngdpbase.database.provider": "sqlite",
  "ngdpbase.addons.forms.enabled": true,
  "ngdpbase.addons.accounting.enabled": true,
  "ngdpbase.addons.accounting-receivables.enabled": true,
  "ngdpbase.addons.accounting-donations.enabled": true
}
```

Set `NGDPBASE_DATABASE_KEY` in the site's environment so the databases are encrypted. Invoices also need the accommodation add-on for the units, owners and billing addresses.

## Backups

Each add-on keeps its records in its own database file in the site's data folder (`accounting.db`, `accounting-receivables.db`, `accounting-donations.db`). Every site backup includes them.
