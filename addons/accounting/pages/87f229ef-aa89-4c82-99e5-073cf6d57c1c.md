# Using Accounting

This page ships with the __accounting__ add-on and is seeded into [{$applicationname}]
when the add-on is enabled.

Accounting keeps the organization's funds and a ledger of money in and out. A posted
entry is never edited or deleted: a mistake is voided, with a reason, and replaced, so
the record of what happened stays.

## Turn it on

The add-on keeps its data in the site's database, so the database must be on:

```json
{
  "ngdpbase.database.provider": "sqlite",
  "ngdpbase.addons.accounting.enabled": true
}
```

Set `NGDPBASE_DATABASE_KEY` in the site's environment so the database is encrypted.

## Who can do what

The add-on ships a __treasurer__ role. It needs a strong sign-in (a passkey), and voids and
transfers ask for a fresh sign-in. Grant the role to the people who keep the books.

## Showing the ledger on a page

The `Ledger` plugin draws the ledger live for the person viewing the page. Nothing is copied
into the page, and each reader sees only what their permissions allow.

| Markup | Shows | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[{Ledger view='balances'}]` | Each fund's opening balance, money in, money out and balance, with a total | `ledger-summary-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='entries' max='20'}]` | The latest entries, newest first (`max='0'` for all) | `ledger-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='entries' fund='<fund id>' voided='true'}]` | One fund's entries, including voided ones | `ledger-read` |

A page drawn by a plugin is kept for up to five minutes per reader, so a new entry can take a
few minutes to appear there.
