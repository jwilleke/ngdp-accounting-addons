# Using Accounting

This page ships with the __accounting__ add-on and is seeded into [{$applicationname}]
when the add-on is enabled.

Accounting keeps the organization's funds and a ledger of money in and out. A posted
entry is never edited or deleted: a mistake is voided, with a reason, and replaced, so
the record of what happened stays.

## Turn it on

Accounting needs ngdpbase 5.17.0 or later, and its forms add-on for the treasurer's screens.

The add-on keeps its data in the site's database, so the database must be on:

```json
{
  "ngdpbase.database.provider": "sqlite",
  "ngdpbase.addons.accounting.enabled": true
}
```

Set `NGDPBASE_DATABASE_KEY` in the site's environment so the database is encrypted.

## Who can do what

The add-on ships a __treasurer__ role. It needs a strong sign-in (a passkey), and voids and
transfers ask for a fresh sign-in. Grant the role to the people who keep the books.

## The treasurer's pages

With the __forms__ add-on enabled, the add-on ships these pages. Only the treasurer can see them and
use their forms, and only an administrator can change them.

| Page | For |
| --- | --- |
| [Accounting] | The treasurer's home: balances and a link to every task |
| [Ledger] | Fund balances and the latest entries |
| [Record an Entry] | Money in or out of a fund |
| [Move Money Between Funds] | A transfer; the total stays the same |
| [Void an Entry] | Void a posted entry with a reason, and post its replacement |
| [Manage Funds] | Create, rename and close funds |
| [Monthly Summary] | Last month and this month, with CSV downloads |

Moving money and voiding ask for a fresh sign-in when yours is more than a few minutes old. The form
shows a __Sign in again__ link; after signing in you come back to the page and submit again.

The entry form offers the categories the site sets in `ngdpbase.addons.accounting.categories`
(a UCOA account code and its label for each).

## Showing the ledger on a page

The `Ledger` plugin draws the ledger live for the person viewing the page. Nothing is copied
into the page, and each reader sees only what their permissions allow.

| Markup | Shows | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[{Ledger view='balances'}]` | Each fund's opening balance, money in, money out and balance, with a total | `ledger-summary-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='entries' max='20'}]` | The latest entries, newest first (`max='0'` for all) | `ledger-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='entries' fund='<fund id>' voided='true'}]` | One fund's entries, including voided ones | `ledger-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='summary' month='previous'}]` | A period: per fund start, in, out and end; per category income and expense | `ledger-summary-read` |
| `[{Ledger view='summary' from='2026-01-01' to='2026-06-30' export='true'}]` | Any period, with CSV download links | the downloads need `ledger-export` |

`month` is `current`, `previous` or `YYYY-MM`. To show the board the summary without the download
links, put it on a page whose `audience` lists the board, without `export='true'`, and grant the
board `ledger-summary-read`: the summary shows no names.

The figures are always current: after every change to the ledger, pages that show it, and the
fund choices in the forms, are drawn again on their next view.
