# Record a Bounced Check

The bank returned a check you deposited. Pick the check, the date the bank took the money back, and the bank's fee. The form fills in the site's penalty for the payer; change it, or set it to 0.

[{Ledger view='form' id='check-bounce'}]

The receipt stays as it was received. The check's amount comes back out of the bank account it was deposited into, in the receipt's own funds and categories; the fee is recorded as bank charges. The payer then owes the check, the fee and the penalty: see [Bounced Checks].
