# Deposits

Each deposit is one line on the bank statement: the receipts taken from Cash on Hand to the bank together. Its number (D-0001 onwards) and the slip reference are what you match against the statement.

## Not yet cleared

Deposits the bank statement does not show yet. When it does, mark each one on [Mark a Deposit Cleared].

[{Ledger view='deposits' cleared='false'}]

## All deposits

[{Ledger view='deposits'}]

A cleared mark put on by mistake is taken off on [Take Off a Cleared Mark]. A deposit entered wrongly is undone on [Undo a Deposit], with the reason; its receipts go back to Cash on Hand, ready for a new deposit.
