# Deposits

Each deposit is one line on the bank statement: the receipts taken from Cash on Hand to the bank
together. Its number (D-0001 onwards) and the slip reference are what you match against the
statement.

[{Ledger view='deposits'}]

A deposit entered wrongly is undone on [Undo a Deposit], with the reason; its receipts go back to
Cash on Hand, ready for a new deposit.
