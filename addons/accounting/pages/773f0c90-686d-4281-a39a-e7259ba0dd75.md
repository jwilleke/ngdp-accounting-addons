# Mark a Deposit Cleared

When the bank statement arrives, find each deposit's line on it and mark that deposit cleared, with the date the bank shows. Nothing moves: the money is already in the account. Its receipts then count as cleared.

[{Ledger view='form' id='deposit-clear'}]

Marked the wrong one? Take the mark off on [Take Off a Cleared Mark]. The deposits still waiting on the bank are listed at the top of [Deposits].
