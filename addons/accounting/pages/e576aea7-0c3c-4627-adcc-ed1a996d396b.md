# Void an Entry

A posted entry is never edited or deleted. To correct a mistake, void the entry with a reason. To put the right figures in its place, fill in the replacement as well: the void and the replacement are posted together.

[{Ledger view='form' id='ledger-void'}]

A transfer is voided as a pair, both funds at once, and has no replacement; record a new transfer instead.
