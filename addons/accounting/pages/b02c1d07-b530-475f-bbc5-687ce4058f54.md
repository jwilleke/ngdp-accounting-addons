# Accounting

The treasurer's home: the money today, and every task one click away.

## Fund balances

What the money is for.

[{Ledger view='balances'}]

## Account balances

Where the money sits. Added up, the accounts equal the funds.

[{Ledger view='accounts'}]

## Money in and out

- [Record a Receipt]: money received (cash, a check, a bank transfer), numbered R-0001 onwards
- [Refund a Receipt]: money returned to the payer, all or part
- [Record an Entry]: money paid out of a fund
- [Move Money Between Funds]: a transfer; the total stays the same
- [Void an Entry]: correct a mistake, with a reason (a receipt is then recorded again)

## Deposits

- [Make a Deposit]: cash and checks taken to the bank together, numbered D-0001 onwards
- [Deposits]: what went to the bank, each with its receipts, and what the bank has not shown yet
- [Mark a Deposit Cleared]: the bank statement shows it
- [Take Off a Cleared Mark]: a deposit marked cleared by mistake
- [Undo a Deposit]: a deposit entered wrongly; its receipts go back to Cash on Hand

## Bounced checks

- [Record a Bounced Check]: the bank returned a deposited check
- [Bounced Checks]: what each payer owes and has repaid
- [Repay a Bounced Check]: money from the payer toward one

## Funds and accounts

- [Create a Fund]: a new fund, with its opening balance
- [Rename a Fund]: a new name or description; the entries stay
- [Close a Fund]: no new entries; the history stays
- [Set an Account's Opening Balance]: what a bank account or Cash on Hand held when the books started

## Closing the books

- [Lock a Period]: after reporting it to the board, so its figures stay true
- [Unlock a Period]: rarely; with the reason
- [Choose How Periods Are Locked]: by month, quarter, half-year or year

## Invoices and donations

On a site that turns on these add-ons:

- [Invoices]: each unit's charges (assessments, rent or dues), one invoice per billing address a month; prepare and issue them on [Prepare This Month's Invoices], record what was paid on [Record a Payment on an Account], and see each unit's balance on [Account Balances]
- [Donations]: donations recorded on receipts, in-kind donations, pledges, donors and year-end [Donation Statements]

## Reports

- [Ledger]: balances and the latest entries
- [Monthly Summary]: last month and this month for the board, with CSV downloads
