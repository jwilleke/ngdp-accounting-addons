# Record a Receipt

Record money received: dues paid in cash, a check from a donor, a bank transfer. Every amount that comes in is a receipt.

[{Ledger view='form' id='receipt-record'}]

One receipt can have several lines: a check paying dues and a donation is one receipt with two lines, each in its own fund and category. Use __+ Add line__ for each.

Each receipt gets a number, R-0001 onwards, to write on the paper slip you give the payer. Numbers are never reused.

- __Entered wrongly?__ Void it on [Void an Entry], with the reason, and record it again. The new receipt gets a new number; the voided one stays in the ledger, marked voided.
- __Money given back?__ That is a refund: [Refund a Receipt].

See [Using Accounting].
