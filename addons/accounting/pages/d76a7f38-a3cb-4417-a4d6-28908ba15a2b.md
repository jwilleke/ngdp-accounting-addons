# Record a Receipt

Record money received: dues paid in cash, a check from a donor, a bank transfer. Every amount that
comes in is a receipt.

[{Form id='receipt-record'}]

Each receipt gets a number, R-0001 onwards, to write on the paper slip you give the payer. Numbers
are never reused.

- __Entered wrongly?__ Void it on [Void an Entry], with the reason, and record it again. The new
  receipt gets a new number; the voided one stays in the ledger, marked voided.
- __Money given back?__ That is a refund: [Refund a Receipt].

See [Using Accounting].
