# Lock a Period

[{Ledger view='lock'}]

After you report a period to the board, lock it. Nothing dated in it can then be added, voided or corrected, so the figures the board saw stay true. A mistake found later is fixed with an entry dated today, with its reason.

[{Form id='period-lock'}]

Choose month, quarter, half-year or year on [Choose How Periods Are Locked]. Rarely, a lock has to come off: [Unlock a Period].
