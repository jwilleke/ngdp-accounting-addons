# Make a Deposit

Taking cash and checks to the bank: tick the receipts that go in, and enter the total from the deposit slip. As you tick, the form shows the running total and whether it matches the slip.

[{Ledger view='form' id='deposit-make'}]

The deposit gets a number, D-0001 onwards; write it on the slip. It appears on [Deposits], and the money moves from Cash on Hand to the bank account. A deposit entered wrongly is undone on [Undo a Deposit].
