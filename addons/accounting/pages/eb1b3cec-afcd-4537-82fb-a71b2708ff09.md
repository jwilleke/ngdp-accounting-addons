# Repay a Bounced Check

Money from the payer toward a bounced check. It is recorded as one receipt, with its own R-number, whose lines fill in order: first the check (in its original category), then the bank's fee (set against bank charges), then the penalty (other income). Anything short stays owed on [Bounced Checks].

[{Form id='bounce-repay'}]

A new check from the payer is recorded here like any other payment.
