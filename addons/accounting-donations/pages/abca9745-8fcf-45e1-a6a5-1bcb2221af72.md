# Donations

Gifts to the organization, recorded as receipts with a gift line. How donations work, and what the
IRS asks for, is in the donations guide that ships with the accounting add-ons.

- [Record a Gift]: a gift received, with any goods or services the donor got in return
- [Add a Donor]: a person or organization with no login, so their gifts reach their statement
- [Pledges]: promises to give, with what has been given and what is left
- [In-kind Gifts]: goods or services given, described but not valued
- [Ledger]: every receipt, with its R-number; open a receipt's slip to print it for the donor

- [Giving Statements]: each donor's year, and their year-end statement
