# Record a Gift

Record a gift received. It becomes a receipt, numbered R-0001 onwards like every receipt, with a
gift line in the fund and donation category you choose.

[{Form id='gift-record'}]

If the donor received goods or services in return (a dinner, a ticket), enter what they were and
their value. For a gift over $75, the receipt slip then shows how much is deductible, as the IRS
requires at the time of the gift. Print the slip from the receipt's link, at
`/api/accounting/receipts/<receipt id>/slip`.

See [Donations].
