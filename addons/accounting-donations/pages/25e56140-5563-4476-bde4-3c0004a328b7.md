# Add a Donor

A donor who has no login on this site: a person, or an organization. They join the site's contacts,
and appear in the donor list on [Record a Gift], [Pledges] and [In-kind Gifts]. Everyone with a login
is already a contact.

[{Form id='donor-add'}]

Give a postal address: the year-end statement goes there. If the donor signs up later with the same
email, the site offers to join their account to this record.

See [Donations].
