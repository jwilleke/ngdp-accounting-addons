# Giving Statements

Each donor's gifts for the year, and their year-end giving statement. A statement is the donor's
written acknowledgment for every gift of $250 or more, so send them in January.

[{Donations view='statements'}]

- [Issue Statements]: every donor still without one, or one donor; email or print
- [Reissue a Statement]: one marked "needs reissue", because a gift changed after it was issued

The organization's name, tax ID and address, and the wording, are site settings: check them with
your accountant before the first statements go out.

See [Donations].
