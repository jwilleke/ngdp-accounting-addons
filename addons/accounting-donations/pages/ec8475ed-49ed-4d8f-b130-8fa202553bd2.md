# In-kind Donations

Donations of goods or services rather than money: a donated laptop, food for an event, a printer's
free work. Describe each one; do not value it. Under IRS rules the organization does not put a value
on a non-cash donation. In-kind donations do not touch fund balances, and appear on the donor's year-end
statement as described items.
